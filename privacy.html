Privacy Policy

Jimothy the Chess Raccoon Discord bot · Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains what the Jimothy Discord bot stores when you use it. It is written for a small self-hosted chess bot, not a large company.

1. Who runs this

The bot is operated by the person who invited it to your Discord server and who runs the host program on their computer (Discord: moralitycore).

2. What we collect

  • Discord identifiers: user ID, username, and the channel or DM where you talked to the bot.
  • Game data: moves, game results, skill settings, and Elo ratings keyed to your Discord ID or local name.
  • Messages you send the bot: commands such as !chess, chat directed at Jimothy, and local-board chat if you use the desktop window.
  • Technical logs: timestamps and error lines on the host machine so the bot can be restarted.

We do not ask for your email, phone number, or payment card. We do not scrape your whole Discord account.

3. Why we use it

To run chess games, draw the board, keep ratings between games, answer you, and (if xAI is enabled) let Jimothy research or talk. Stored lessons live in files on the host such as data/xai-brain.json, data/mind.json, and data/ratings.json.

4. xAI and other processors

If an xAI API key is configured, text you send Jimothy for talk or study may be sent to xAI so Grok can reply or search the public web. When xAI credits run out, the bot falls back to lessons already stored on the host. Chess engine moves use Stockfish on the host, not xAI, unless a play-via-xAI option is on.

Discord also processes messages under Discord’s Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for ads.
  • We do not share it except with Discord (to deliver messages) and xAI (only when those features are on).

6. How long we keep it

Ratings and game files stay on the host until the operator deletes them. Channel messages the bot posted may be deleted after a game if that cleanup is enabled. You can ask the operator to delete your Elo entry and stored chat lines.

7. Your choices

Stop using the bot, kick it from the server, or message moralitycore on Discord and ask to erase your user ID from ratings and memory files. You can also use Discord’s own privacy tools.

8. Children

The bot is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child gave us data, contact the operator and we will delete what we have.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.

10. Contact

Discord: moralitycore, on the server where Jimothy is installed.
